What changes for you

Three things, after every ride.

Know what you did right

The clean corners, the well-judged braking, the sections where you were precise. The review starts from what works, so you can recognise it and repeat it.

Know exactly what to fix

Not “slow down”. Precise points: where you braked too early, where you got on the throttle late, and a single priority for your next ride.

See progress, ride after ride

Every session is compared with the ones before it on the same kind of road. Smoothness, braking, line: numbers going up, not vague feelings.
