Hardware

The APEX kit is covered by the statutory two-year legal guarantee for consumers in the EU.

What's covered

Manufacturing defects and failures under normal use.

What's not

Damage from crashes, improper fitting not carried out by APEX or its partners, water ingress beyond the stated rating, or modification of the kit.

How to claim

Write to hello@apex-rider.com with your order details and a description of the issue.
