FAQ

Pricing questions

What exactly is included in the price?

Every APEX pack includes three things in one price: the kit, installation on your bike and 12 months of reviews. No monthly subscription and no hidden costs: pay once, ride all year.

Who can buy APEX Start at €49.90?

APEX Start is reserved for riders under 25 or licensed for less than 12 months. The contents are the same as the Rider pack: kit, installation and 12 months of reviews.

How does payment work?

There is no payment on the site. You fill in the early access quick form, we contact you, we confirm the pack and compatibility with your bike together, and only then do we handle payment and delivery.

Is the early access price guaranteed?

Yes. Requesting early access locks in your pack price and delivery priority on the first kit run, which is limited.

When will APEX Pro be available?

APEX Pro (€179.90, built for track and track days) will soon be available for pre-order. Leave your contact in the early access form to be notified at opening.

What happens after the 12 included months?

There is no automatic renewal: we do not store payment methods. Before the end we present your options and you decide whether to continue.
