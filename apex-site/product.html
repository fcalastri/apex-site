01 · The review

Anatomy of a review

A

Your line vs the ideal line

Every corner shows where you actually went and where the best line ran. The gap between the two is your lesson.

B

Braking and throttle, point by point

Where you started braking, where you released, when you got back on the throttle and how progressively. Not impressions: points on the map.

C

What worked

The clean corners and the right calls, highlighted in green. Improving starts with repeating what you already do well.

D

One single priority

Every review closes with one thing — one — to work on next time out. That's how a real coach works: one goal at a time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does APEX work on my motorcycle?

APEX is designed to work on any motorcycle, from nakeds to big adventure bikes. When you request early access we contact you and confirm compatibility with your model before any payment.

Do I have to look at anything while riding?

No. APEX shows nothing while you ride and requires no interaction in the saddle: zero distractions. The review lands on your phone when you get off the bike.

Does it work on both road and track?

Yes. The review works on the road, on mountain passes and on track. For track days with advanced lap-by-lap analysis there is the APEX Pro pack.

What happens after the 12 included months?

The pack price includes the kit, installation and 12 months of reviews. There is no automatic renewal: before the end we present your options and you decide whether to continue.

Who can see my reviews?

Only you. Reviews are personal: no mandatory public leaderboard, no score visible to others. Details on how data is handled are in the privacy notice.
